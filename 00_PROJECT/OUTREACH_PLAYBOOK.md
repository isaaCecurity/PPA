# PPA Strategic Outreach Playbook

Each company is treated as its own campaign.

1. Research company and current hiring/PPA signals.
2. Select two humans: Security/IT and Talent/HR.
3. Review recent LinkedIn activity before contacting them.
4. Send a concise LinkedIn connection request where appropriate.
5. After acceptance, send a short personalized PPA DM.
6. If they provide a route/referral, use that context in the email.
7. Otherwise send the tailored email through the verified route.
8. Attach the company-appropriate CV.
9. Track every confirmed action in the company Activity Log and master tracker.
10. Follow up after 5–7 business days.
11. Escalate only when justified.

`STARTPPA` will later mean: check relevant Gmail replies, classify responses, update the master tracker and company activity logs, and identify the next action.
